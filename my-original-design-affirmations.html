The Mirror of My Original Design

Affirming the Finished Work in Christ

Identity & Origin

I am found in Christ. I was included in Him before the foundation of the world, and I was found in Him before I was ever "lost" in Adam.

I am the architect's dream. I am God's cherished love dream realized and brought to fruition.

I am defined by my Source. My identity is not based on my behavior or the "I-am-not-tree," but on the image and likeness of Elohim.

I am a trophy of grace. I am the evidence of God's success, His glorious trophy, and His allotted heritage.

Relationship & Proximity

I live face-to-face with the Father, unhindered by guilt, suspicion, blame, or inferiority.

I am a dovetail joint of peace. I am joined to God in a union so strong that nothing can separate us; I am "at one" again.

I am highly favored and greatly endeared. God's love for His Son is His love for me. I am accepted in the Beloved.

I am the coming of age of God's heart. I am not a legal orphan, but a son who has come into my full inheritance.

Power & Provision

I am energized by the mind and muscle of God. The same force that raised Jesus from the dead is dynamically at work within me right now.

I am a part of the fullness. The completeness of His being that fills all in all resides in me.

I am flooded with light. The eyes of my inner mind are illuminated. I am "glowing from knowing" the truth of my redeemed design.

I am the measure of God's wealth. I am God's portion, the sum total of His assets, and the measure of His wealth.

"The days of window-shopping are over. In him every face is unveiled. In gazing with wonder at the blueprint of God displayed in human form, we suddenly realize that we are looking into a mirror."

Inspired by the Mirror Bible, Ephesians Chapter 1.
